Purpose and scope
This policy sets binding rules on “Compliance” for Marenthal Chemicals Inc.. It applies to all employees,
managers and, where contractually agreed, to business partners. Violations may have consequences
under employment and criminal law.
Obligations and responsibilities
This document is maintained in the document management system of the Marenthal Group and is
binding in its currently valid version.
